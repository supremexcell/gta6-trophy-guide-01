⚠ READ THIS FIRST — THE ONE TROPHY YOU CAN MISS

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only possibly-missable trophy on the list. Early in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia mentions meditation in the kitchen at Jason’s house one morning and heads out to the balcony — follow her and accept the prompt. That balcony meditation raises your counter but is not marked as a spot like the others, and it cannot be repeated after Chapter 1. Every other trophy can be earned in free roam after the story.

Sources: PushSquare, KeenGamer, GameMapa, GTADB — all four flag this same moment. Whether the balcony session is strictly required is still unconfirmed, so do it and remove the doubt.

ROADMAP TO THE PLATINUM ~200 hours · difficulty 4/10 · 1 playthrough · all offline

  1. Phase 0 — Before you start

    Keep cheats off on your Platinum save — cheats disable trophies and saving. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots). Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation above.

    Covers: Positive Mental Attitude

  2. Phase 1 — Story first 60–80 hours

    Play the campaign through all five chapters and enjoy it. Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study animals through your binoculars as you go — the 100% checklist later wants 200 of 277 vehicle models and 100 of 255 animals. Pick up optional missions (purple icons) when they appear.

    Covers: 12 story trophies · You Gotta Know When to Get Off

  3. Phase 2 — Side stories & strangers

    Finish the character questlines — Andy (5 quests, Lucia, Ch. 3), Kenton (7 shipwrecks, Ch. 1), Billy (5 quests, Mud Club, Ch. 2), Randy (16 wish-list cars, Ch. 3), Tyrone (labs, Jason, Ch. 3), Bret’s hunt — and complete 8 Strangers & Freaks strands. These are not on the 100% checklist, so do them now or they will be all that is left.

    Covers: Road to Rehabilitation · This Was No Boat Accident · The Lone Rider · Supply & Demand · Spin On This! · Fair Game · Strangers & Freaks

  4. Phase 3 — Activities

    Gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges, win Top Fun drone races at all 14 locations, win 20 different races (30 for 100%), land 250 of the 397 stunts, beat Cal at fishing, and finish one arcade game. Try all 9 activity types once for Master of None.

    Covers: The Shootist · Top Fun · Earn Cash, Not Kudos · Cunning Stunts · Baited My Tackle · Stick Fiddler · Master of None

  5. Phase 4 — Crime & money

    Rob 5 bank vaults (8 for 100%), one illegal operation per gang (9 gangs, 39 operations total), armored trucks and jewelry stores, escape 4-star heat with your partner, launder $250,000 in marked bills, spend $100,000 in shops, buy all 8 garages ($160,000) and register 10 vehicles with Scooter Bros.

    Covers: Maximum Withdrawal · Pest Control · A Classic Blitz Play · Sneakthief · Hot Together · Retail Therapy · Dime on the Dollar · Treat Yo Self · Little Boxes · On the Spot

  6. Phase 5 — Collectibles & cleanup

    Finish 3 card sets (15 for 100%), scan 100 WAiNK locations, meditate at all 18 spots, discover 100 vehicles, explore all 52 areas of Leonida, complete 50 of the 80 Challenges (70 for 100%), then open Progress > 100% Completion and clear whatever is left. A Long Road pops — and with it, the Platinum.

    Covers: Don’t Touch My Deck · You WAiNKer! · Positive Mental Attitude · Whip It Good · The Goodtime State · One Last Thing · A Long Road · Vice Got a Price!

Time and difficulty estimates: PowerPyx via GameMapa / KeenGamer (~200 hours, 4/10). The 100% checklist only asks for part of most activities — e.g. 15 of 18 card sets, 300 of 397 stunts — so do not hunt literally everything.
